Future You — Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026

Future You is a life-trajectory calculator that runs entirely on your device. This policy is short because the app collects nothing.

What the app stores

The answers you type in (age, household, income, savings, debt, spending, work hours, sleep, exercise and similar) and the scenarios you pick are saved only in the app's local storage on your phone so they are there the next time you open it. They are never transmitted anywhere.

What the app sends

Nothing. Future You makes no network requests. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party SDK that collects data. The only outbound links are the ones you tap yourself (this policy and jonathanscribbles.com), which open in the system browser.

Sharing

If you tap Share on your letter, the text is handed to the iOS share sheet and goes only where you send it.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. Everything it stored goes with it. You can also overwrite your answers at any time with Edit.

Children

Future You is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Not advice

The projections are estimates computed from your own answers using a transparent set of assumptions shown inside the app. They are not financial, medical or legal advice.
