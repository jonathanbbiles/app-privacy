ScootSteps — Privacy Policy & Support

Last updated: 1 August 2026

Learn to line dance with animated, top-down footwork diagrams. Ten full dances, every foundation step, and a count track that keeps you on the beat.

Support

Questions, bugs, or a dance you'd like added? Email jonathanbbiles@gmail.com. I read every one.

The short version

ScootSteps doesn't collect anything about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party advertising or analytics SDK of any kind.

What ScootSteps stores

Your streak, which dances you've marked as "learning" or "know it cold", your practice history, and your settings (count sound, haptics, text size) are saved in the app's own storage on your device. That information is never transmitted to me or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

The one thing that leaves your phone

Every dance, diagram and count tick is built into the app and works with no signal. The exception is the song list on a dance page: if you tap Preview, the app sends that song's title and artist to Apple's public iTunes Search API (itunes.apple.com) in order to play Apple's own 30-second preview and to link to the song on Apple Music or Spotify. Nothing about you or your device is included in that request, and no identifier is attached to it. Apple's handling of that request is governed by Apple's privacy policy. If you never tap Preview or a song link, ScootSteps makes no network requests at all.

Music

ScootSteps does not play copyrighted recordings. The audio you hear during practice is a count track synthesized by the app. Song titles are listed as references so you can practise to the real record in your own music app.

In-app purchases

There are none. ScootSteps is free, and every dance in it is included — nothing is locked, and the app never asks you for money.

Children

ScootSteps is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes
