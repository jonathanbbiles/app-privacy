Frequently asked
How do I build a loop?
Pick a key at the top, then tap chords to hear them — each tap adds that chord to your loop. Every chord offered belongs to the key, so they all sound good together. Drag a step to reorder it, tap ✕ to remove it.
What do Major, Minor and 7ths do?
Major is bright, minor is moody. 7ths swaps the three-note chords for four-note ones, which sounds jazzier and softer.
Can one chord play differently from the rest?
Yes. How the chords play sets the default for the whole loop. Any single step can override it — tap the small style button under that chord.
What is the difference between Export audio and MIDI?
Export audio makes a real sound file you can send to anyone. MIDI drops the notes straight into GarageBand or Logic so you can keep writing.
Where are my saved ideas kept?
On this device, in this app. Each saved idea keeps its own key, sound and tempo — opening one puts every control back the way you left it.
What does Pro unlock?
Ten more instruments, both drum patterns, and MIDI export. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
Sound not working?
Tap for a one-second test tone. If you hear it, audio is working — including with the ring/silent switch on.
