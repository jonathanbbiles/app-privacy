Closerly Terms and Disclaimer

Effective 13 September 2026

Closerly provides conversation prompts for entertainment and connection. The app does not provide therapy, counselling, medical, psychological, legal, or professional relationship advice, and it is not a substitute for any of them.

Who Closerly is for

Closerly is intended for consenting adults aged 18 and over who are in a relationship with each other. It is not a dating, hookup, chat, matchmaking, or social platform. It has no profiles, no messaging, and no way to reach anyone outside the room you are in.

The cards

Every card is an invitation, not an instruction. Any prompt may be skipped by either person at any time, for any reason or none, and nothing in the app records or penalises a skip. The cards contain no dares, no scoring, and nothing that requires either person to do anything.

The After Dark deck is optional, sold as part of Closerly Pro, and sits behind a separate agreement screen. Its prompts are about desire, appreciation and preference, and are written to be answered in words. Nothing in the deck instructs anyone to do anything to anyone.

Use your own judgement

Conversations about feelings, history and desire can be difficult as well as good. You know your relationship and the app does not. If a prompt is not right for you, skip it. If a conversation raises something that needs real support, speak to a qualified professional.

Purchases

Closerly Pro is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. It is not a subscription and does not renew. Payment is processed by Apple; prices may vary by region. Purchases can be restored on any device signed in with the Apple ID used to buy them.

Your data

Everything the app stores stays on your device. Closerly does not back it up for you. Deleting the app, or using Delete All Local Data, permanently removes it.
