Renoly Privacy Policy

Effective 13 September 2026

Renoly is designed to work without user accounts, advertising, tracking, or online data collection.

What Renoly collects

Nothing. Renoly does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information. Project measurements, estimates, contractor quote details, notes, and settings are stored locally on your device and are not sent anywhere.

The app makes no network requests of its own. It contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporting SDK, no advertising identifier, and no remote configuration. After you download it, every calculator, the budget planner, the quote comparison and PDF export work with the radios switched off.

Purchases

If you choose to buy Renoly Pro, payment is processed by Apple through the App Store. Renoly does not receive or store your payment card information. The app records only whether Apple reports the purchase as owned on this device.

Sharing a PDF

PDF reports are generated on your device. When you choose to export or share one, the app opens Apple's own sharing interface. The destination you pick — Mail, Messages, Files, or another app — is controlled by you and is subject to that service's privacy practices. Renoly does not upload the PDF anywhere.

Support

If you contact support by email, your email provider may process the information you choose to send. Support emails are used only to respond to your request.

Deleting your data

Settings includes Delete All Local Data, which permanently removes every saved project, quote, setting, and custom default from the device. Deleting the app removes the same data.

Children

Renoly is a general-audience planning tool. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If Renoly's data practices change in a future version, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before the change takes effect.
