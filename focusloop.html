Privacy Policy

Pomodrift: Minimalist Habit Widget · last updated 13 September 2026

The short version

Pomodrift does not collect anything. There is no account, no server, and the app makes no network requests. Your timer settings and your record of finished rounds are stored on your iPhone and nowhere else.

What is stored, and where

Your phase lengths, the state of the current timer, your finished rounds and whether you have bought Pomodrift Pro are kept in a shared container on your device. The app and its home-screen widget both read from it — that is how the widget can start and pause the timer without opening the app. Nothing in that container leaves the device, and it is removed when you delete Pomodrift.

Purchases

Pomodrift Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. The app asks the App Store whether your Apple ID owns it, and stores the yes-or-no answer locally. Payment details are never seen by the app or by its developer. Apple's own privacy policy covers that transaction.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

There are none. No analytics SDK, no advertising, no crash reporter, no third-party code that phones home. Pomodrift does not track you across apps or websites and has no advertising identifier to do it with.

Notifications

If you allow them, Pomodrift schedules a local notification for the moment the current phase ends, so it can chime while you are in another app or the screen is locked. It is scheduled and delivered entirely on your device; nothing is sent to a server, and there are no push notifications. Declining is fine — the timer still works, it just cannot make a noise when you are not looking at it.

Children

Pomodrift is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. A change that involved collecting anything would also be described in the app's release notes.
