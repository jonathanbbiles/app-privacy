Dueline Privacy Policy

Effective 13 September 2026

Dueline is designed to work without user accounts, bank connections, advertising, tracking, or online data collection.

What Dueline collects

Nothing. Dueline does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information. The bills you add, their amounts, due dates, categories, notes, payment history, and your settings are stored locally on your device and are not sent anywhere.

The app makes no network requests of its own. It contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporting SDK, no advertising identifier, and no remote configuration. After you download it, adding bills, the totals, the category breakdown, the forecast, and the reminders all work with the radios switched off.

There is no bank connection

Dueline does not connect to a bank, a card, a payment provider, or any account-aggregation service. It contains no Plaid, Open Banking, or similar integration, and it never asks for a banking credential. Every figure in the app is one you typed in yourself. Dueline cannot read, pay, cancel, or change anything on your behalf.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by iOS on your device. There is no push notification service, no server, and no message sent over the internet. The reminder text is assembled on the device from the bill you entered. If you decline the notification permission, the rest of the app works exactly as before — it simply cannot remind you.

Currency

Dueline never converts between currencies. It has no connection to fetch an exchange rate, and it does not carry one. The currency you choose in Settings changes how amounts are displayed and nothing else.

Purchases

If you choose to buy Dueline Pro, payment is processed by Apple through the App Store. Dueline does not receive or store your payment card information. The app records only whether Apple reports the purchase as owned on this device.

Export

CSV export creates a file on your device and hands it to Apple's own sharing interface. The destination you pick — Mail, Files, another app — is controlled by you and is subject to that service's privacy practices. Dueline does not upload the file anywhere.

Support

If you contact support by email, your email provider may process the information you choose to send. Support emails are used only to respond to your request.

Deleting your data

Settings includes Delete all local data, which permanently removes every bill, its payment history, and your settings from the device. Deleting the app removes the same data. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, there is nothing left to delete afterwards — and nothing to restore, so export first if you want a copy.

Children

Dueline is a general-audience utility. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If Dueline's data practices change in a future version, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before the change takes effect.
