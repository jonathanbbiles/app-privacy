Dueline Terms and Disclaimer

Effective 13 September 2026

Dueline is a personal record-keeping and reminder tool. The app does not provide financial, investment, tax, accounting, legal, or debt advice, and it is not a substitute for any of them.

It tracks what you type in

Every amount, date, and cycle in Dueline is one you entered. The app has no connection to your bank, your card, or any provider; it does not verify that a bill exists, that an amount is correct, or that a payment was made. It cannot pay, cancel, pause, or change anything on your behalf. If a figure in the app is wrong, it is wrong because the figure entered was wrong, and only you can correct it.

Averages are averages

Monthly and yearly figures are arithmetic averages, not payments. A yearly bill is spread across twelve months in the monthly figure and arrives all at once in reality — that is exactly why the forecast exists. A weekly bill is counted 4.35 times a month, because there are 52.18 weeks in an average year. Use the figures for planning, not for reconciling an account.

Currency

Dueline does not convert between currencies and carries no exchange rate. The currency setting changes how amounts are displayed and nothing else. If you enter amounts in more than one currency, the totals will add numbers that are not comparable.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications delivered by iOS. Whether and when they appear depends on your device: notification permission, Focus modes, Do Not Disturb, Low Power Mode, a device that is off, and iOS's own limit on how many pending notifications an app may hold. Dueline keeps the soonest reminders when that limit is reached. Treat a reminder as a convenience, not as a guarantee — missing a payment remains your responsibility.

Purchases

Dueline Pro is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. It is not a subscription and does not renew. Payment is processed by Apple; prices may vary by region. Purchases can be restored on any device signed in with the Apple ID used to buy them.

Your data

All data you enter stays on your device. Dueline does not back it up for you and cannot recover it. Deleting the app, or using Delete all local data, permanently removes it. Export to CSV if you want a copy.

Liability

Use of Dueline is at your own risk. Jonathan Biles is not responsible for missed payments, late fees, overdrafts, cancelled services, losses, damages, or decisions resulting from use of the app or from a reminder that did or did not arrive.
