Slow Burn — Privacy Policy & Support

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Slow Burn is a guided intimacy program built on Sensate Focus — six unhurried stages you move through on your own, or with a partner. It is a wellness and education tool, not medical advice.

Support

Questions, bugs, or anything at all? Email jonathanbbiles@gmail.com. We read every one.

The short version

Slow Burn collects nothing about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party advertising or analytics SDK of any kind. Nothing you enter is ever sent to us, because there is no server of ours for it to be sent to.

What stays on your device, always

All of this is saved only in the app's own storage on your phone, and is never transmitted — not to us, not to anyone:

  • Your name and pronouns, and anything you entered about your partner
  • Your gender, orientation, relationship structure and body-area wording preferences
  • How many sessions you have logged, and at which stage
  • The free-text private note you write after each session

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Pairing two phones — end-to-end encrypted

The only feature that uses the network at all is live pairing, where two people run the program together on their own phones. It exists so the two phones can do two things: open the next stage only when both people have privately confirmed they are ready, and show each of you the reflections you both named.

That link is end-to-end encrypted. When you pair, the two phones perform an ephemeral ECDH P-256 key exchange, publishing only their public keys. Each phone derives the shared secret itself — the secret is never transmitted, in any form — and that secret, combined with your pair code, produces an AES-256-GCM key that only your two phones hold. The pair code itself never travels. Everything sent afterwards is ciphertext.

What crosses between the two phones, encrypted: a number from 0 to 6 for the stage you have confirmed you are ready for, and the positions of the reflection chips you tapped (sent as list positions, never as words).

What never crosses at all, encrypted or otherwise: your name, pronouns, gender, orientation, relationship structure, session counts, and every written private note.

The messages are carried by a public relay (broker.emqx.io) that only ever sees scrambled bytes it cannot read, and keeps none of them once you disconnect. We do not operate that relay, we receive nothing from it, and it stores nothing after a session ends.

The safety word

Once your two phones have agreed on their key, each shows the same six-character safety word. If it reads the same on both phones, no one is in the middle of your connection. Checking it is optional and takes a second.

What we cannot hide, honestly

Encryption hides the contents of your messages, not the fact that messages exist. Someone watching the public relay could see that two anonymous parties are exchanging encrypted messages: when they connect and disconnect, roughly how often, and roughly how large the messages are. None of that is tied to a name, a device, or any other session, and none of it reveals a single thing you chose or wrote. We would rather say this plainly than claim more than we can deliver.

In-app purchases

Slow Burn is free and nothing in it is ever locked. There is an optional tip jar if you would like to support the program; tips are handled entirely by Apple, and we receive no payment or customer information from them.

Not medical advice

Slow Burn is a wellness and education program. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it is not a substitute for care from a qualified clinician. If pain during intimacy, a medical condition, a recent surgery, or a trauma history is involved, please work with a licensed clinician — a sex therapist or a pelvic-floor physical therapist — before or alongside this program. The app links to independent directories to help you find one; we are not affiliated with them.

Age

Slow Burn is intended for adults. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.
