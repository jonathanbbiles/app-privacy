Streakling Pro

Streakling Pro is a one-time purchase that removes the three-habit limit and unlocks the large widget and history. It is tied to your Apple ID and can be restored on any device signed in to it. Ticking habits off, the home-screen widget and your streaks are free and are intended to stay free.

Purchases are made through the App Store and are subject to Apple's terms. Refunds are handled by Apple, not by the developer — request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

If you stop being a Pro user, habits beyond the free limit are hidden and shown locked. They are not deleted, and they come back if you buy Pro again.
