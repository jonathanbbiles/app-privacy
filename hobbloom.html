Hobbloom Privacy Policy

Effective 24 September 2026

Hobbloom helps you find and start a new hobby. It does that without knowing anything about you.

What Hobbloom collects

Nothing. Hobbloom does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information. It has no account, no sign-in, no server, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK, and no advertising identifier. It does not track you across apps or websites.

What is stored on your device

The hobbies you save and the first steps you tick are kept in the app's local storage on your phone. Your searches are processed on the device and are not stored or sent anywhere. You can erase everything at any time with About (the ⓘ button) → Delete all local data, or by deleting the app.

The Amazon links

Each starter-kit item has a button that opens an Amazon search in your phone's browser. The link carries our Amazon Associates tracking ID. As an Amazon Associate, Hobbloom earns from qualifying purchases — a small commission paid by Amazon, at no extra cost to you. That is how the app is paid for.

Hobbloom itself sends Amazon nothing about you, and it never learns what you view or buy. Once you tap a link you are on Amazon's website or app, and what Amazon collects there is governed by Amazon’s own privacy notice, not this one.

Permissions

Hobbloom asks for no device permissions: no camera, photos, location, contacts, microphone or notifications.

Children

Hobbloom collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If Hobbloom ever started collecting anything, the App Store privacy label would change first.
