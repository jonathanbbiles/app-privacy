Suspish Terms and Disclaimer

Effective 13 September 2026

Suspish is an educational tool that reads text you give it and points out patterns commonly found in fraudulent messages. It is not a security product, not antivirus, not a filter, and not a fraud-prevention service.

It does not decide whether something is a scam

Suspish reports what it found and what that usually means. It does not and cannot determine whether a message is fraudulent, whether a sender is who they claim to be, or whether a link is dangerous. It reads the text and nothing else.

There is no guarantee of accuracy in either direction. A sophisticated fraudulent message may produce no findings at all, and a genuine message may produce several. A result of "Nothing found" or "Little found" is not an assurance that a message is safe, and the app says so every time it shows one.

Always verify independently

Whatever Suspish reports, do not use a link, phone number, or address taken from a message you are unsure about. Contact the organisation using details you already had — from your card, a statement, their official app, or a number you looked up yourself. That habit defeats almost everything this app looks for, with or without the app.

It cannot act for you

Suspish cannot block a sender, report a message, cancel a payment, contact your bank, or change anything on your device or your accounts. It shows you information and stops there.

No professional advice

Nothing in Suspish, including the Learn section, is legal, financial, or security advice. If you have lost money, contact your bank immediately and report it to your country's fraud reporting service.

Purchases

Checking a message — including every indicator found and the recommended next step — is free and unlimited, and will remain so. Suspish Pro is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase that adds unlimited history and a shareable explanation. It is not a subscription and does not renew. Payment is processed by Apple; prices may vary by region. Purchases can be restored on any device signed in with the Apple ID used to buy them.

Your data

Everything the app stores stays on your device, and only a short excerpt of each checked message is kept. Suspish does not back anything up and cannot recover it. See the Privacy Policy.

Liability

Use of Suspish is at your own risk. Jonathan Biles is not responsible for losses, damages, costs, or decisions resulting from use of the app, from a message the app did not flag, or from a genuine message the app did flag.
