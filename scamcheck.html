Suspish Privacy Policy

Effective 13 September 2026

Suspish is built so that the message you are worried about never leaves your phone. That is not a policy choice bolted on afterwards — it is the reason the app exists.

The message you check

The text you paste into Suspish is analysed entirely on your device, by rules shipped inside the app. It is never sent anywhere. There is no server to send it to, no cloud analysis, no AI service, no reputation or blocklist lookup, and no network request of any kind. The app works with your phone in Airplane Mode, which is the simplest way to confirm this for yourself.

What Suspish collects

Nothing. Suspish does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information. It has no account, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no crash reporting SDK, no advertising identifier, and no advertising of any kind.

What is stored on your device

If history is switched on — it is on by default, and one tap in Settings turns it off — Suspish saves, on this device only:

  • A short excerpt of each message you checked, no more than 80 characters. The full message is never stored.
  • The risk level and the indicators that were found.
  • The date of the check.

Nothing else. The message currently in the text box is not written to storage at all; it is gone when you clear it or close the app.

Turning history off

Settings → Keep a record. With it off, nothing at all is saved and the app works exactly the same way. If you are checking something you would rather not have a trace of, that switch is there for you.

Deleting your data

Delete a single check from its detail sheet, delete all history from the History tab, or use Delete all local data in Settings to remove everything including your settings. Deleting the app removes the same data. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, there is nothing left afterwards.

Purchases

If you choose to buy Suspish Pro, payment is processed by Apple through the App Store. Suspish does not receive or store your payment card information. The app records only whether Apple reports the purchase as owned on this device.

Sharing an explanation

Suspish Pro can copy or share a plain-English explanation of what was found. That explanation contains the findings and the advice — not the message you checked. Where you send it is entirely your choice and is subject to that service's privacy practices.

Support

If you contact support by email, your email provider may process the information you choose to send. Please do not paste a suspicious message into a support email; we do not need it, and sending it defeats the point of the app.

Children

Suspish is a general-audience utility. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If Suspish's data practices change in a future version, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before the change takes effect.
