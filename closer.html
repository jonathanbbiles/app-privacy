Closerly Privacy Policy

Effective 13 September 2026

Closerly is designed to work without user accounts, advertising, tracking, or online data collection.

What Closerly collects

Nothing. Closerly does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information. Your favourite cards, which cards you have already seen, and your settings are stored locally on your device and are not sent anywhere.

The app makes no network requests of its own. It contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporting SDK, no advertising identifier, and no remote configuration. After you download it, every deck works with the radios switched off.

The age confirmation

Closerly asks you to confirm that you are 18 or over before the decks open, and asks for a second, separate confirmation before the After Dark deck opens. These are declared confirmations. The app does not ask for, collect, or store a date of birth, an age, a name, or any other identifying information, and it does not verify identity.

No accounts, no messaging, no other people

Closerly has no profiles, no sign-in, no matching, no messaging, no comments, and no user-generated content. It is a deck of cards for two people in the same room. The app has no means of contacting anyone or of sharing anything about you with anyone.

Purchases

If you choose to buy Closerly Pro, payment is processed by Apple through the App Store. Closerly does not receive or store your payment card information. The app records only whether Apple reports the purchase as owned on this device.

Support

If you contact support by email, your email provider may process the information you choose to send. Support emails are used only to respond to your request.

Deleting your data

Settings includes Delete All Local Data, which permanently removes your favourites, your deck progress, and your settings from the device. Deleting the app removes the same data.

Children

Closerly is rated 17+ and is intended for adults. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If Closerly's data practices change in a future version, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before the change takes effect.
