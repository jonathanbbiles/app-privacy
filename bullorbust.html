Bull or Bust — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Bull or Bust collects no data. Full stop. The app is an educational trading simulator that runs entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no network calls to us. The app has no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. Your simulated trades, scores, and settings are stored only on your device (in on-device storage) and never leave it.

Third parties

None. The app does not send information to any third party. In-app purchases, if offered, are handled by Apple under Apple’s own privacy terms; we never see your payment details.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and safe for all ages. Because it collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here.

Support & Contact

Bull or Bust is an educational simulator. Markets are randomly generated on your device — there are no real prices, no brokerage or exchange, and no real money. Nothing in the app is financial advice.
