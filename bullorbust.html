Bull or Bust — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Bull or Bust collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. The app never sends any information to its developer.

Learn mode

Learn mode is entirely on-device. Markets are randomly generated on your iPhone — there are no real prices, no brokerage, and no real money. Your simulated trades, scores, and settings are stored only on your device and never leave it.

Live mode (Bull or Bust Pro)

Live mode connects to your own free Alpaca paper-trading account, using API keys that you create at Alpaca and enter yourself. When Live mode is in use:

  • The app talks directly to Alpaca (paper-api.alpaca.markets and data.alpaca.markets) to read market data and to place, list, and cancel paper orders. The real-money trading host is never contacted.
  • Your Alpaca API key and secret are sent only to Alpaca, only in request headers — never in a URL, never logged, and never to us or to any third party.
  • Your keys are stored on your device in the iOS Keychain. "Disconnect & remove keys" in the Account tab erases them.
  • What you do in Live mode is governed by Alpaca's own terms and privacy policy.

Live mode places paper trades only. No real money is ever at risk, and the developer of Bull or Bust never sees your account, your keys, or your trades.

Third parties

Other than Alpaca — contacted only in Live mode, and only with credentials you supply — the app sends information to no one. In-app purchases are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy terms; we never see your payment details.

Children

The app is rated 4+. Because it collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here.
