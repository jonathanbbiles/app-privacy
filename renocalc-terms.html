Renoly Terms and Planning Disclaimer

Effective 13 September 2026

Renoly provides general planning calculations and organizational tools. The app does not provide construction, engineering, architectural, financial, legal, code-compliance, permitting, contractor-selection, or safety advice.

Estimates are estimates

All calculations are estimates based on measurements you enter and assumptions you select. Actual material requirements, product coverage, installation methods, local requirements, labor costs, waste, site conditions, permits, and contractor scope may vary.

Before purchasing materials, beginning work, or hiring a contractor, verify measurements, manufacturer specifications, building-code requirements, permit obligations, insurance, licenses, contracts, and final scope of work with appropriate qualified professionals.

Quote comparison

The quote comparison displays what you enter. It identifies the lowest total you have entered and which entry has the most fields filled in. It does not evaluate, endorse, recommend, or rank contractors, and a lower bid may exclude materials, permits, cleanup, warranty, or scope of work that another bid includes.

Purchases

Renoly Pro is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. It is not a subscription and does not renew. Payment is processed by Apple; prices may vary by region. Purchases can be restored on any device signed in with the Apple ID used to buy them.

Your data

All data you enter stays on your device. Renoly does not back it up for you. Deleting the app, or using Delete All Local Data, permanently removes it.
