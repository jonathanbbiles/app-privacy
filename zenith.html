Privacy Policy

Streakling · last updated 13 September 2026

The short version

Streakling does not collect anything. There is no account, no server, and the app makes no network requests. Your habits and your record of them are stored on your iPhone and nowhere else.

What is stored, and where

The habits you create, the days you have marked them done, and whether you have bought Streakling Pro are kept in a shared container on your device. The app and its home-screen widget both read from it — that is how tapping the widget can tick a habit off without opening the app. Nothing in that container leaves the device, and it is removed when you delete Streakling.

Purchases

Streakling Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. The app asks the App Store whether your Apple ID owns it, and stores the yes-or-no answer locally. Payment details are never seen by the app or by its developer. Apple's own privacy policy covers that transaction.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

There are none. No analytics SDK, no advertising, no crash reporter, no third-party code that phones home. Streakling does not track you across apps or websites and has no advertising identifier to do it with.

Children

Streakling is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. A change that involved collecting anything would also be described in the app's release notes.
